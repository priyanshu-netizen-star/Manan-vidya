# User manuals

Short, task-based guides for each group of users. The application works in any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer. On a phone, tap **Menu** (bottom right) to see all sections.

**Signing in:** open the School OS address, choose **Sign in**, enter the email and password the school gave you. After five wrong passwords the account is locked for 15 minutes. Use **Sign out** (top right) on shared devices.

---

## Everyone: AI Copilot, search and theme

- **AI Copilot.** Press **AI Copilot** (bottom right) or **Ask AI** (top bar) and type a question such as "What needs my attention today?" or "How do I request an outpass?". It answers from your own information and this guide, and shows links to the right pages. It never makes decisions for you.
- **Search.** Press **Ctrl K** (or the search box at the top) to jump to any page, find a student, enquiry, topic or lesson you are allowed to see, or send the question to the Copilot.
- **Briefing.** Your dashboard lists what needs attention today. Press **AI briefing** for a short written summary.
- **Theme.** The sun/moon button switches between light, dark and your device's setting.

## 1. Students

### Study a topic
1. Open **Learn & practise**. Your subjects and topics for your class are listed.
2. Tap a topic. Read the material your teacher has approved.

### Ask a question
1. On the topic page, type your question in **Ask a question** and tap **Ask**.
2. Read the explanation and the parts of the material it came from.
3. AI can make mistakes. If something seems wrong, ask your teacher.
4. Never type your phone number, address or other personal details.
5. If something is worrying you, talk to a teacher, your class teacher, the warden or a parent. You can also call **CHILDLINE 1098**.

### AI study assistant

Open **AI study assistant**, choose a topic, then choose what you want: **Ask anything**, **Explain simply**, **Step by step**, **Revision notes**, **Flashcards** (press a card to see the answer) or **Quiz me** (practice questions, not marked). It uses only your teacher's approved material. Never type personal details.

### Take a quiz
1. On the topic page, tap **Start** next to a quiz (or **Retake** to try again).
2. Choose one answer for each question, then tap **Submit answers**.
3. You see your score straight away. Read the explanation under each question you got wrong.

After submitting a quiz, press **Explain my mistake** under any question you got wrong.

### Check your progress
Open **Progress**. Each topic shows your best score and a band: Not started, Needs support (below 50%), Developing (50–79%) or Secure (80% and above).

### Coding, AI and robotics
1. Open **Coding, AI & robotics** and choose a lesson.
2. Read the lesson, then do the challenge:
   - **Python / JavaScript / HTML:** write your code and tap **Run and check**. Green ticks mean a check passed. Tap **Hint** if you are stuck, **Reset** to start again. The first Python run takes a little longer while Python loads.
   - **Robot:** write commands such as `forward 2`, `left`, `right`, `repeat 3 { forward }` and tap **Run robot**. Reach the star without hitting a wall.
   - **AI literacy:** answer the questions and tap **Check answers**.

In any code or robot challenge, press **Ask the AI tutor** for a hint. It will point you to the problem but will not give you the answer.

### Hostel (boarders)
- **Maintenance:** report a problem in your room (category, description, priority).
- **Leave & outpass:** see your requests and their status. Requests are usually made by your parent.

## 2. Parents and guardians

### See your child's progress
**Dashboard** and **Progress** show topic results and recent quizzes. These are practice results to support learning, not exam marks.

### Report cards
Open **Report cards**. A report card appears once the school has finalised the exam. Tap **View**, then **Print** to print or save as PDF.

### Request leave or an outpass (boarders)
1. Open **Leave & outpass**.
2. Choose **Leave** (overnight or home) or **Outpass** (same day).
3. Enter leaving and returning date and time, where your child is going, who will collect them, and the reason. Tap **Submit request**.
4. The status shows **Awaiting warden** until the warden decides. If a request is rejected, the warden's reason is shown.
5. You can **Cancel** a request while it is still awaiting the warden.

### Admission enquiries (for new families)
Open the **Admissions help** page (no sign-in needed). Ask questions in English or हिंदी, leave an enquiry and keep the reference number (for example `MV-7KQ2XD`). Check progress any time with the reference and your mobile number.

## 3. Teachers

### Your workspace
**Teacher workspace** shows your classes and subjects, items waiting for review, and quick links. Use **Add a topic** for a new topic in a subject you teach.

### Write and approve learning material
1. **Learning material** → **Write new material** → choose the topic, add a title and content → **Save as draft**.
   - Leave a blank line between paragraphs. You can use simple Markdown (headings `##`, lists `-`, bold `**text**`).
2. Open the item to check the preview, then **Approve for students**.
3. Editing approved material sends it back to draft, so approve it again after checking.

**Draft with AI:** on **Learning material**, choose a topic and press **Draft with AI**. The draft is marked "AI draft". Check every fact, edit, then approve.

### Build the question bank
1. **Question bank** → **Write a question**: the question, four options (select the correct one), an explanation students will see after the quiz, and difficulty → **Save as draft**.
2. **Approve** each question after checking it.
3. **Draft questions with AI** (if AI is enabled): choose the topic, number and difficulty. Drafts appear marked **AI draft**. Check every one carefully, edit if needed, then approve.
4. A question that students have already answered cannot be edited; write a new one instead.

### Create and publish a quiz
1. **Quizzes** → **New quiz**: choose class and topic, give it a title → **Create draft quiz**.
2. Tick the approved questions to include → **Save quiz**.
3. **Preview as student** to check it.
4. **Publish to class**. Students of that class see it immediately.
5. Open the quiz any time to see: how many students attempted, the percentage correct for each question with the most common wrong answer, each student's scores, and who has not attempted. **Close quiz** hides it from students and keeps the results.

### Worksheets
**Worksheets** → choose class and topic → **Choose** → give a title, tick questions → **Save as draft** → **Approve**. Open a worksheet to print it; **Show answer key** prints the answers for you (students never see the key).

### Prepare a lesson
**Lesson preparation** → choose class and topic, length and an optional focus → **Draft with AI** (or **Build template** if AI is not enabled). Edit the plan, set the status to **Reviewed** when you have checked it, and **Print** if needed. Plans are private to you.

### Class performance
**Class performance** shows each topic's average score (lowest first) and students below 50% on any topic. Use **View as table** for exact numbers. Treat these as a prompt for conversation: one quiz is a small sample.

Press **Find misconceptions and what to re-teach** for an AI summary of which wrong answers students chose most often and what to re-teach.

### Attendance
**Attendance** → choose class and date → **Open register**. Everyone starts as **P** (present); tap **A** (absent), **L** (late) or **E** (excused) for exceptions → **Save register**. You can correct a saved register by saving again.

### Marks
**Exams & marks** → choose the exam → choose your subject → type marks (leave blank if not entered) → **Save marks**. Marks over the maximum are highlighted and cannot be saved. Once the office locks an exam, marks are read-only.

---

**Report-card remarks:** open a student's report card, press **Suggest a remark**, edit it, then **Save draft** or **Approve remark**. Families see only approved remarks.

## 4. Office and admissions staff

### Daily enquiry routine
1. Open **Enquiries** → **Follow-up due** and **Escalated** first.
2. Open an enquiry. Escalated enquiries show why the assistant did not answer (for example a fee question). Contact the parent personally.
3. Update the status (**Contacted**, **Visit / interaction scheduled**, **Application submitted**, **Admitted** (note required), or **Closed**), set the next **Follow-up** date, assign an **Owner**, and add notes. Everything appears on the timeline.
4. When an escalation has been answered, use **Mark escalation handled** with a short note.

On an enquiry, press **Draft reply** for a suggested message in the parent's language. Copy it, edit it and send it yourself; nothing is sent automatically.

### Keep the assistant's answers correct
1. Open **Assistant knowledge base**. Each entry shows its verification status.
2. Open an entry to edit the English and Hindi question and answer, keywords (words parents might use, comma-separated), and source.
3. Set **Verification** to **Confirmed by the school** only when the principal or authorised staff has confirmed the answer. Tick **Hindi text reviewed** only after a fluent staff member has checked it. Tick **Published** to make it available.
4. Check **Questions the assistant passed to staff** each week and add approved answers for common questions.
5. Never publish fee amounts unless the school has approved them for publication.

### Student records
**Student records** → filter or search → open a student. **Add a student** or edit a record (admission number, class, roll, guardian, boarder). Set status to **Inactive** when a student leaves.

### Exams and report cards
1. **Exams & marks** → **Create an exam** (name, class, date, maximum marks per subject).
2. Teachers enter marks. The **Marks entered** column shows progress.
3. When marks are final, **Lock** the exam. Report cards become final and visible to parents. **Unlock** only to correct an error (it is recorded).
4. **Report cards** → choose the exam → open a student → **Print**.

### Reports and tasks
- **Reports**: class strength, attendance (30 days), students below 85% attendance, latest exam results, enquiry pipeline, quiz participation. Use **CSV** to download for Excel.
- **Tasks**: create tasks for yourself or colleagues, update status, and note what is blocking a task.

---

**Ask your data:** at the top of **Reports**, type a question such as "Which students have low attendance?". The answer names the report it used; open the full report from there. **Explain this report** summarises the report on screen.

## 5. Hostel wardens

### Leave and outpass
1. **Leave & outpass** shows open requests, awaiting decisions first.
2. **Approve**, or type a reason and **Reject** (the parent sees your reason).
3. When the student leaves with the named escort, tap **Check out**. When they return, tap **Mark returned**.
4. You can also enter a request on a parent's behalf (for example after a phone call).

### Roll-call
**Roll-call** → date and **Morning** or **Night** → **Open**. Students with approved leave are already marked **On leave**. Mark anyone **Absent** → **Save roll-call**. If anyone is absent, follow the school's missing-student procedure immediately.

### Rooms and beds
**Rooms & beds** shows every bed and occupant. Use **Allocate or move a student** to give a boarder a free bed (choosing a student who already has a bed moves them). **Vacate** frees a bed and keeps the history.

### Maintenance
**Maintenance** lists open repairs first. Update the status (**In progress**, **Resolved**, **Closed**) and who is handling it. Raise requests for any room.

## 6. Principal and management

### Command centre
- Use the **Class** and **Period** filters at the top. Key figures: attendance, enquiries needing action, average practice-quiz score, hostel status.
- Charts: attendance trend, enquiries by status, lowest-scoring topics, tasks with overdue items. Hover for values; **View as table** for exact figures.
- **Search records** finds students, enquiries and tasks.

The **Executive briefing** and **Ask your data** panels at the top of the command centre summarise the day and answer questions from the standard reports.

### Oversight
- **Enquiries**, **Reports**, **Class performance**, **Hostel overview** and **Tasks → All tasks** give detail behind the dashboard.
- **Learning material** and **Question bank**: management can approve content.
- **Audit log**: who did what and when.

---

## 7. Platform administrator (VoxVision support or school IT)

- **Users & roles**: create accounts (students and parents must be linked to a student record), change roles, deactivate leavers (they are signed out immediately), and see what each role can do.
- **System & AI status**: environment, database, AI configuration and the go-live checklist.
- **Audit log**: review regularly, especially exports and unlocks.
- Presentation environment only: **Reset presentation data** restores the sample records and signs everyone out.
