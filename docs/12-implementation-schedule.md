# Twelve-week implementation schedule

The 12 weeks begin after the agreement is signed, the first payment is received, and the school has named a project lead and staff contacts. Dates move if prerequisites (approvals, data, access) are delayed; any change is agreed in writing.

| Weeks | Phase | VoxVision delivers | School provides | Exit criteria | Payment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Discovery and design | Requirement workshops with principal, office, teachers and wardens; review of current software; data-protection plan; confirmed scope, acceptance criteria and pilot classes; hosting set-up | Project lead; staff time for workshops; current software details and vendor contact; decisions on AI and hosting | Signed-off requirements and acceptance criteria | **40% (₹1,40,000) at project start** |
| 3–5 | AI learning pilot | Subjects, topics and teaching assignments for pilot classes; teacher workspace; material and question bank loaded with teachers; quizzes live; AI configured if approved | Pilot teachers; approved material and questions; AI service account (if chosen) | Pilot teachers publish quizzes; pilot students use them | |
| 5–6 | Admissions assistant | Knowledge base completed with school-confirmed answers in English and Hindi; enquiry workflow and staff training; public page on the agreed address | Confirmed admissions information; Hindi reviewer; written authorisation to collect enquiries | Assistant answers confirmed questions; enquiries tracked end to end | **30% (₹1,05,000) at core pilot review** |
| 6–8 | Administration workflows | Student records (import if available), attendance, exams, report cards with the school's grade scale, reports, tasks; ERP option agreed in discovery | Student, class and staff data (or export); grading scale; exam structure | Office and teachers complete a register, a marks entry and a report-card print | |
| 8–10 | Hostel management | Rooms, beds and boarders; leave and outpass approvals; roll-call; maintenance | Room list; boarder list; leave policy; safeguarding procedure | Warden completes leave, roll-call and maintenance flows | **20% (₹70,000) at module review** |
| 10–11 | Testing and training | User acceptance testing against the agreed criteria; security review; fixes; training sessions per role; user manuals | Staff for testing and training | Acceptance criteria met; training attendance recorded | |
| 12 | Handover | Go-live readiness check; production switch-over (sample data removed); documentation and credentials handover; start of initial support | Sign-off | Handover certificate signed | **10% (₹35,000) at final handover** |

## Milestone reviews

Each payment milestone has a review meeting where the school checks the relevant features against the acceptance criteria in *Testing and acceptance criteria*. Issues found are logged with a priority and a fix date.

## Assumptions

- Pilot covers the classes agreed in discovery (for example Classes VI–VIII); wider roll-out follows the pilot.
- The school provides content and data in usable form (spreadsheets or exports).
- Staff are available for workshops, testing and training as scheduled.
- Third-party dependencies (AI service, hosting, messaging, ERP vendor) are arranged by the school or agreed separately.

## Key risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Delay in confirming admissions information | Assistant goes live only with confirmed answers; others are escalated to staff |
| ERP vendor does not allow integration | School OS works standalone; one-time import or file exchange as fallback |
| Low adoption by staff | Role-based training, short manuals, champions in each department, pilot first |
| AI cost uncertainty | Usage measured during the pilot; hourly limits; AI optional |
| Data-protection concerns | Approved plan before real data; minimal data; audit log |
