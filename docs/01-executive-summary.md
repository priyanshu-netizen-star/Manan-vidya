# Executive summary

**VoxVision School OS** is the school technology platform that VoxVision proposes for **Manan Vidya Manrakhan Mahto School, Ranchi**. It brings learning, teaching, admissions, school administration, the hostel and management reporting into one connected system, used through a web browser on phones, tablets and computers.

> This is a proposal. The school has not commissioned, approved or endorsed it. The working application shown with this proposal uses only synthetic (invented) sample records and is not connected to any school system.

## The problem it addresses

Like most schools, the school's day-to-day work runs across many separate places: registers, spreadsheets, phone calls, paper slips and the existing school software. That makes it hard to see the whole picture, easy for a parent's enquiry or a hostel request to be missed, and slow to prepare reports and report cards. Students also have limited support when they study outside class.

## What the school gets

| Module | What it does, in one line |
|---|---|
| AI learning platform | Students read teacher-approved material, ask questions, take quizzes that are marked instantly and track their progress. |
| Teacher workspace | Teachers prepare material, questions, quizzes, worksheets and lesson plans, publish to classes and see topic-wise results. |
| Admissions and parent assistant | Parents get admission answers in English or Hindi from school-approved information; enquiries are tracked to completion. |
| School administration | Student records, attendance, marks, printable report cards, tasks and reports, with role-based access. |
| Hostel management | Beds, leave and outpass approvals, morning and night roll-call, and maintenance requests. |
| Coding, AI and robotics | Hands-on Python, web, AI-literacy and virtual robot activities with project briefs. |
| Management command centre | One screen for the principal and management across all of the above. |

## How it works together

Information is entered once and used everywhere. A quiz a teacher publishes feeds the student's progress page, the teacher's class report and the management dashboard. A parent's leave request flows to the warden, then into roll-call and the hostel summary. An enquiry from the assistant becomes a tracked follow-up for the office and a number on the principal's dashboard.

## Responsible use of AI

- AI only **drafts**: lesson plans and questions are saved as drafts that a teacher reviews and approves.
- Answers come from **approved sources**: the learning assistant explains from material teachers approved; the admissions assistant answers only from school-confirmed information and hands anything else to staff.
- **People decide**: no admission, discipline, welfare or hostel decision is made automatically.
- **Child safety**: messages suggesting a child may be at risk are not sent to AI or stored; the student is directed to a trusted adult and CHILDLINE 1098.
- **Honesty**: when AI is not switched on, the screen says so and shows approved material instead.
